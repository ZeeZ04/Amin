About Amin

Amin runs on this computer. Your questions, documents and conversations are stored here and nowhere else.

When your documents hold the answer, Amin quotes them and shows the passage. Otherwise it answers from general knowledge and says so. For medical, legal or financial questions it tells you who to ask instead of advising.
