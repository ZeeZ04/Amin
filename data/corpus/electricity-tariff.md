# Slab rates

Residential consumption is billed in three slabs. The first 2,000 kWh per month
is charged at 23 fils per kWh. Consumption from 2,001 to 4,000 kWh is charged at
28 fils per kWh. Consumption above 4,000 kWh is charged at 38 fils per kWh.

# Fixed charges

A monthly meter charge of AED 10 applies to every residential account
regardless of consumption. A one-time connection fee of AED 2,000 applies to new
residential connections.

# Billing and payment

Bills are issued monthly on the 5th of the month and are due within 21 days of
issue. A late payment penalty of 2 percent of the outstanding balance is applied
after the due date. Accounts more than 60 days overdue are subject to
disconnection, and reconnection carries a fee of AED 300.

# Security deposit

The security deposit for an apartment is AED 1,000 and for a villa AED 2,000.
The deposit is refunded within 45 days of account closure.
