# Cover limits

The total contents sum insured is AED 150,000. Individual items valued above
AED 5,000 must be listed separately on the schedule to be covered. Portable
electronics are covered away from the home up to AED 10,000 in aggregate per
period of insurance.

# Excess

A standard excess of AED 750 applies to each claim. The excess for escape of
water claims is AED 1,500.

# Exclusions

The policy excludes loss or damage arising from wear and tear, mechanical
breakdown, and any property used for business purposes. Cash is excluded beyond
AED 2,000.

# Claims

Claims must be notified within 30 days of the incident. Theft claims require a
police report reference number.
