# Term and rent

Clause 3.1 — The tenancy runs for 12 months commencing 1 March 2026 and expiring
28 February 2027. Clause 3.2 — The annual rent is AED 78,000, payable in four
equal cheques of AED 19,500.

# Notice and renewal

Clause 7.1 — Either party must give not less than 90 days written notice before
expiry of their intention not to renew. Clause 7.2 — Where no notice is served,
the contract renews automatically on the same terms for a further 12 months.

# Maintenance

Clause 9.1 — The landlord is responsible for major maintenance, defined as
structural repair and the central air conditioning system. Clause 9.2 — The
tenant is responsible for minor maintenance up to AED 500 per incident,
including light fittings, door handles and tap washers.

# Security deposit

Clause 11.1 — The security deposit is AED 3,900, equal to 5 percent of the annual
rent. Clause 11.2 — The deposit is refundable within 30 days of handover, less
the cost of any damage beyond fair wear and tear.
