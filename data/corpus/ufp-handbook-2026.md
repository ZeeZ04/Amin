# Assessment weighting

The PHY-104 module is assessed by coursework worth 30 percent and a final
written examination worth 70 percent. Coursework comprises three laboratory
reports of equal weight.

# Examination rules

The final examination lasts 2 hours. A data and formula booklet is provided in
the examination hall; students may not bring their own copy. Non-programmable
calculators are permitted. The pass mark for the module is 40 percent.

# Attendance

Students must attend at least 80 percent of scheduled laboratory sessions to be
eligible to sit the final examination. Absence supported by documentation is
excluded from the calculation.

# Resits

A student who fails PHY-104 at first attempt may resit the written examination
once. The resit mark is capped at 40 percent for the purpose of the module
grade.
