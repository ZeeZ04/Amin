# Warranty

The ArcticLine FR-440 carries a 24-month parts and labour warranty from the date of
purchase. The sealed cooling system, comprising the compressor, condenser and
evaporator, is covered for 60 months. Warranty service requires the original
proof of purchase and the serial number printed on the inner left wall of the
fresh food compartment.

The warranty does not cover damage caused by power surges, transport by the
customer, or use of non-ArcticLine water filters.

# Error codes

Error code E1 indicates a fresh food compartment thermistor fault. Error code E4
indicates that the defrost heater circuit is open. Error code E7 indicates a
communication failure between the main board and the display panel; unplug the
appliance for 5 minutes before calling service.

# Temperature settings

The recommended fresh food compartment setting is 4 degrees Celsius. The
recommended freezer setting is minus 18 degrees Celsius. After installation,
allow 24 hours before loading food so the cabinet reaches its set point.

# Water filter

Replace the water filter cartridge, part number ALF-9, every 6 months or after
every 1500 litres dispensed, whichever comes first. The filter indicator light
turns amber at 90 percent of cartridge life.
