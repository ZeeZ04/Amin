# Coverage period

The Vertex V14 carries a 12-month limited hardware warranty from the date of
purchase. The battery is covered for 6 months or 500 charge cycles, whichever
comes first. An extended warranty may be purchased within 30 days of purchase
for AED 450, taking total coverage to 36 months.

# What is not covered

The warranty excludes liquid damage, cracked displays, damage from
non-Vertex power adapters, and any unit with a removed or altered serial number.
Software and data loss are not covered.

# Service process

Warranty service begins with a support reference number obtained online. The
standard turnaround is 7 working days from receipt at the service centre.
Out-of-warranty diagnostic assessment costs AED 120, credited against the repair
if the repair is accepted.

# Battery health

A battery is considered defective under warranty if it retains less than 80
percent of rated capacity within the coverage period.
