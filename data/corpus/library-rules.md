# Borrowing limits

Foundation and undergraduate students may borrow up to 8 items at a time.
Postgraduate students may borrow up to 15 items. Standard loan period is 14
days. Short-loan items are issued for 3 days and cannot be renewed.

# Renewals and fines

Standard loans may be renewed twice, provided no other reader has reserved the
item. Overdue standard items incur a fine of AED 2 per item per day, capped at
AED 50 per item. Overdue short-loan items incur a fine of AED 10 per item per
day with no cap.

# Reservations

A reserved item is held at the collection desk for 3 working days after
notification. Readers may hold a maximum of 5 active reservations.

# Group study rooms

Group study rooms may be booked for up to 3 hours per day per reader and require
a minimum of 3 occupants. A booking not claimed within 15 minutes is released.
