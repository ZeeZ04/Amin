# Allowances

The Freedom 150 plan costs AED 150 per month and includes 20 GB of local data,
500 local voice minutes and unlimited local SMS. Unused data does not roll over
to the following month.

# Out-of-bundle charges

Data beyond the bundle is charged at AED 0.50 per MB up to a monthly cap of
AED 200. Local voice minutes beyond the bundle are charged at 30 fils per minute.

# Roaming

A daily roaming pass costs AED 35 and includes 1 GB of data and 60 minutes of
incoming calls, valid for 24 hours from first use. Roaming without a pass is
charged at AED 2 per MB.

# Contract

The minimum contract term is 12 months. Early termination carries a fee equal to
50 percent of the remaining monthly charges. The SIM replacement fee is AED 25.
