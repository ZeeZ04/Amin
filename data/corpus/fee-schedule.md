# Tuition

Annual tuition for the University Foundation Programme is AED 52,000, payable in
two instalments of AED 26,000. The first instalment is due by 15 August 2026 and
the second by 10 January 2027.

# Other charges

A non-refundable application fee of AED 500 applies. The registration fee is
AED 1,500 per academic year. The examination resit fee is AED 400 per module.
The replacement student identity card fee is AED 75.

# Refunds

A student withdrawing before the start of teaching receives a 90 percent refund
of tuition paid. Withdrawal within the first four weeks of teaching attracts a 50
percent refund. No tuition refund is available after week four. Refunds are
processed within 30 working days of a written withdrawal request.

# Late payment

Payment received after the due date incurs a late fee of AED 250. A student with
outstanding fees exceeding AED 5,000 may not register for the following term.
