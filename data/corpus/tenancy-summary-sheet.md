# Headline terms

Annual rent for Unit 1204 is AED 82,000, payable in four cheques. The tenancy
is a standard twelve-month term.

# Notice

Tenants intending not to renew should give 60 days written notice before the
contract expires.

# Deposit

The refundable security deposit for this unit is AED 4,100.

# Agency fee

The letting agency commission is 5 percent of the annual rent, payable on
signature and non-refundable.
