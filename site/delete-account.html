Delete your account

In the Amin app, open Settings › Account › Delete account. Your account and subscription record are deleted from our servers at once. Your documents and conversations are on your device, so deleting the app removes them.

If you can no longer open the app, email [to fill: support email] from the address on your account and we will delete it within 30 days.

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel it in your store account settings first.
