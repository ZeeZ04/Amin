# D1 - iPhone design spec (on-device Amin)

Principle: calm, sand-and-teal, trust shown rather than claimed. Chrome is system Liquid Glass (toolbar, tab bar, sheets); brand lives in content (door mark, teal citations, saffron accents). Never put glass on glass; never glass behind long text. Saffron is for one thing per screen (the primary orb or the current-source highlight). Body text is system colours, so Dark Mode and Increase Contrast work. Teal `#0E5E5A` on sand passes AA for 17 pt text; on dark use `Color.aminTeal` lightened 20% (add `aminTealLight` to `Kit/Theme.swift`).

Global tokens: 16 pt screen margin, 8 pt grid, cards radius 20 (`.rect(cornerRadius: 20, style: .continuous)`), chips `Capsule`, min tap 44x44. Type is system only, scales with Dynamic Type: title `.largeTitle.bold()`, body `.body` (17), meta `.footnote`/`.caption.weight(.semibold)` (caption never below 12 at default). Never fixed `.system(size:)` except icons (use `@ScaledMetric`).

## 1. Onboarding (new `OnboardingView.swift`, replaces `ConnectView` as the first run; ConnectView stays as "Use my Mac instead", under Advanced)
`NavigationStack` of 4 full-screen pages, `TabView(.page)` with a custom dot row, `aminSand` background (deep teal in dark).
1. **Promise.** `DoorMark` 96 pt, centred, drawn in once (stroke trim 0.6 s). Title "Amin keeps it between us." Body "Your questions, documents and voice never leave this iPhone. No account. No cloud." Three rows (`Label`, 17 pt, SF Symbols `lock`, `doc.text.magnifyingglass`, `wifi.slash`): "Works in airplane mode" / "Every answer shows its source" / "Delete anything, any time". CTA `.buttonStyle(.glassProminent)` tint `.aminTeal`: "Continue".
2. **Model.** Card per option, radio selection. "Amin Standard, 5.7 GB, recommended for this iPhone" (6-bit) and "Amin Plus, 7.4 GB, best quality; needs 12 GB RAM" (shown only if device qualifies, check `ProcessInfo.physicalMemory` and free disk). Under it: "Needs Wi-Fi once. After that, Amin never goes online." CTA "Download 5.7 GB" -> in-place `ProgressView(value:)` linear bar, teal, with "2.1 of 5.7 GB · about 6 min left", Pause/Cancel text buttons, runs as a background `URLSession`. Verify checksum then "Checking the model..." Failure: "Download stopped. Your progress is saved." [Resume]. Low storage: "You need 6.5 GB free. Free up space in Settings > General > iPhone Storage." Mobile data: confirm alert before download.
3. **Microphone.** Pre-permission screen with orb illustration. "Talk to Amin. Your voice is turned into text on this iPhone and is never recorded or sent." Buttons "Allow microphone" (triggers system prompt) and ghost "Not now". Denied state later: orb replaced by "Microphone is off" + "Open Settings".
4. **Ready.** "Amin is ready." Name field (for greeting, optional) and language picker (English, العربية, മലയാളം; defaults from `Locale.preferredLanguages`). CTA "Start". Offer "Say 'Hey Amin'" toggle (off by default; explain battery).
Haptic: `.sensoryFeedback(.success)` at download done. Skip is always available top-trailing on pages 1, 3, 4, never on 2.

## 2. Chat (`ChatView.swift`, `TurnView`, `AnswerView`, `FlowChips`, `SourceView`)
Layout: scroll of turns; composer pinned bottom with `safeAreaBar`/`safeAreaInset` and glass capsule field (min height 44, grows to 6 lines). User bubble: trailing, `aminTeal` fill, white text, radius 20. Answer: no bubble, full width, `.body` with `lineSpacing(4)`, 16 pt margins, 12 pt between paragraphs.
- **Answer header chip (above text, 28 pt tall):** documents answer = teal `doc.text` "From your documents · 2 sources" (existing); general answer = neutral `globe` "General knowledge" with sub-line `.footnote` "Not from your documents. Amin can be wrong." Use a quieter grey fill `.secondary.opacity(0.12)` so documents answers feel earned; do not colour general answers amber/red (red is reserved for conflict and errors).
- **Citations:** inline superscript number tokens (teal, 17 pt min tap height via `.frame(minWidth:44)` hit area using `contentShape`), and below the answer a horizontal `FlowChips` row: `[1] Lease.pdf · p.4` capsule, 12 pt caption, max 2 lines at accessibility sizes (wrap, do not truncate to one line when `dynamicTypeSize.isAccessibilitySize`).
- **Source sheet (`SourceView`):** `.presentationDetents([.medium, .large])`, drag indicator visible. Top: file name `.headline`, "Lease.pdf · Section 4" `.subheadline` grey, page badge. Passage in a sand card (radius 16, 16 pt padding), cited sentence highlighted with a saffron 25% background (`AttributedString.backgroundColor`). Footer actions: "Open document" and "Copy passage". Footnote (current copy OK). Prev/next chevrons to step through sources 1..n.
- Footer row: copy, speak, regenerate (icons 20 pt, 44 pt targets). Latency number in AnswerView: remove from default UI (noise); show only under Settings > Developer.
- **Streaming:** text appears word by word at model speed; no typewriter fake. "Reading your documents..." (step 1) then "Writing..." (step 2) with the door mark pulsing at 0.6 Hz; replace the bare `ProgressView` in `TurnView`.
Fix in code: `Welcome` copy "Runs on your Amin computer" -> "Runs on this iPhone. Nothing you ask leaves it." once local engine is active; make it a switch on `Engine.kind`.

## 3. Voice entry (`VoiceView.swift`, composer mic in `ChatView`)
Entry: mic button in composer (44 pt, `mic`; when text is empty it is the primary, when text exists the send arrow replaces it with a cross-fade 0.15 s). Long-press mic = push-to-talk with waveform in the composer. Tap = full-screen `VoiceView` as `.fullScreenCover` on `aminTealDeep`. Orb 220 pt (saffron core, teal glow); states and copy: "Listening" (orb breathes with input level), "Thinking" (slow rotation), "Speaking" (pulses with audio), "Paused". Captions under orb `.title3` for what Amin heard, `.callout` for reply. Buttons: End (glass capsule, 56 pt tall), mute mic. Privacy caption keeps "Your voice is turned into text on this iPhone." (change "Amin computer"). Errors: "I couldn't hear that. Tap the orb to try again." Haptics: light impact on start, soft on stop, `.success` nothing (silence is fine). Reduced Motion: orb scale/glow static, ring opacity changes only.

## 4. Documents and scanning (`LibraryViews.swift`: `DocumentsView`, `DocumentScanner`)
`List(.insetGrouped)` of documents with leading file-type icon 36 pt in a tinted rounded square, title `.body`, meta `.footnote` "12 pages · added yesterday". Status trailing: "Ready" (teal check), "Reading 40%" (`ProgressView(.circular)`), "Couldn't read" (amber `exclamationmark.triangle`, tap for reason). Top-trailing `Menu` "+" with Scan, Import file, Photo. Scanner is `VNDocumentCameraViewController`; after scan, a review sheet: page thumbnails, auto name "Scan 29 Oct", language picked, "Add to Amin". OCR happens on device via Vision (`VNRecognizeTextRequest`, Arabic is supported; Malayalam not in Vision: flag, show "Malayalam scans may be less accurate"). Swipe left: Delete (confirm: "Delete Lease.pdf and everything Amin remembers from it?"). Empty state `ContentUnavailableView`: "No documents yet" / "Scan a paper or add a file. Amin will answer from it and show you where." with prominent "Scan a document".

## 5. Memory, "Teach Amin" (extract from `SettingsView` into top-level screen `MemoryView`, reachable from Settings and from Chat toolbar)
Header card: "Teach Amin" `.title2.bold()`, "Short facts Amin should remember. You can see, edit and delete every one." Add field + "Remember" button (glass). Facts in `List`: text `.body`, meta source ("You taught it" / "From chat") and date `.footnote`. Swipe: Forget (destructive), Edit (teal). Suggested rows after a chat "Remember that my daughter's school is Al Noor?" with Save / Not now chips (already "Saved to your memory" label exists in `AnswerView`; add Undo for 6 s). Empty state: "Nothing yet. Try: 'Remember that I prefer short answers.'" Haptic `.success` on save, `.impact(.light)` on forget.

## 6. Settings (`SettingsView.swift`)
Order: Profile; **Model** (new section); Voice and language; Memory; Library access ("General chat may use"); Privacy; Storage; About. Model section rows: name "Amin Standard", status pill (Ready teal / Downloading / Update available / Not installed), size, "Last check 3 days ago", buttons "Switch model", "Delete model (frees 5.7 GB)", and a toggle "Download updates only on Wi-Fi". Show live health: "Running on this iPhone · 0.9 s typical". Privacy section: "No account. No analytics. Network used only to download models you choose." plus "Delete everything" (double confirm, red). Remove sections "Amin computer", "Invite someone", and "Sign out" from the default path when running local; keep under "Connect to a Mac" (Advanced) so ConnectView logic isn't lost.

## 7. Empty, loading, error
Empty: `ContentUnavailableView` everywhere (History, Documents, Projects, Memory). Loading: skeleton lines for lists > 0.4 s; avoid spinners over 1 s without text; model load at launch shows door mark + "Waking Amin..." (target under 2 s; keep model memory-mapped). Errors (no jargon, always an action): "Amin couldn't finish that. [Try again]"; out of memory "Amin needs more memory. Close other apps and retry."; model missing "The model was removed. [Download again]". Inline in chat as a card with `exclamationmark.triangle` plus retry; use `alert` only for destructive confirmations. Replace `ErrorAlert` in `MainView.swift` with inline banners (`.safeAreaInset(edge: .top)`, glass capsule, auto-dismiss 5 s but VoiceOver keeps it).

## 8. Accessibility, Dynamic Type, languages
- Dynamic Type through AX5: stacks use `ViewThatFits`; `FlowChips` wrap; composer field grows; the tab bar keeps text labels. Test at Large, XXXL, AX5.
- VoiceOver: answer is one element whose label reads the text, then "Sources: one, Lease.pdf page 4, button" via `.accessibilityElement(children: .contain)` and `.accessibilityAction(named:)`; the label chip reads "From your documents, 2 sources" or "General knowledge, not from your documents". Orb: label by state ("Amin is listening"), value changes announced with `AccessibilityNotification.Announcement`. `DoorMark` is hidden (correct). New answer arrival: post announcement "Answer ready".
- Arabic RTL: use leading/trailing only, never left/right; chevrons use `.flipsForRightToLeftLayoutDirection(true)`. Per message direction by content (existing hack in `Welcome.starterButtons` using unicode scalars -> extract to `View.autoDirection(for:)` and apply in `AnswerView`, `TurnView`, composer). Numbers: keep Western digits as user setting; citation tokens stay `[1]`. Arabic needs +10% line height (`lineSpacing(6)`); never italicise or letter-space Arabic. Add `ar.lproj` and `ml.lproj` String Catalog.
- Malayalam: system font renders via Kohinoor/Malayalam Sangam MN; its glyphs are tall, so use `lineSpacing(6)`, min 17 pt body, no `.caption2` (11 pt) in ml; allow two-line chips; test conjuncts do not clip in `lineLimit(1)` (change to 2). Language chip in the composer lets the person force reply language.

## 9. Haptics and motion
Haptics (`.sensoryFeedback`): send `.impact(flexibility:.soft)`; answer finished `.success`; citation tap `.selection`; voice start `.impact(.light)`; forget/delete `.warning`; error `.error`. None while streaming. Motion: springs `.smooth(duration: 0.35)` for sheet content, `.snappy(0.25)` for chips; list inserts with `.transition(.blurReplace)` guarded by `accessibilityReduceMotion` (fall back to opacity 0.15 s). Matched transition from citation chip to source sheet (`.matchedTransitionSource` + `.navigationTransition(.zoom)`). Orb and logo animation honour Reduce Motion and pause in Low Power Mode.
