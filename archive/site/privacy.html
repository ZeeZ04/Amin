Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

The short version: your questions, documents, memories, conversations and voice stay on your own devices. Our servers hold only your account and your subscription.

Who we are

What stays on your device

Everything you ask, every document you add, the facts you teach Amin, your conversation history and anything you say to it. Amin's language model and speech recognition run on your device. If you connect Amin to your own Amin computer at home, that data goes to that computer over your home network, which you control. We never receive it.

What we hold

  • Account: your email address, a display name if you give one, your interface language, and when the account was created. Passwords are stored by our authentication provider as salted hashes.
  • Subscription: your plan, the product bought, the store it was bought through, and when it renews or ends. Payment details are handled by Apple or Google; we never see them.

We use this only to sign you in, give you the features of your plan, and contact you about your account. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train models.

Service providers

  • Supabase: hosts the account database and sign-in.
  • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions with Apple and Google, linked to your account by an anonymous id.
  • Apple App Store, Google Play: process payments under their own policies.
  • Hugging Face: hosts the model file your device downloads once. Like any download, it sees your IP address.
  • Vercel: hosts this website.

Your rights

You can see and correct your account details in the app, and delete your account at any time in Settings › Account › Delete account or at amin's deletion page. Deleting it removes your account and subscription record from our servers. Data on your devices is removed when you delete the app. Depending on where you live (for example the UK and EU GDPR, or California's CCPA) you may also ask us for a copy of your data or to restrict its use: write to [to fill: support email].

Retention

Account and subscription records are kept while your account exists and deleted when you delete it, except where the law requires us to keep billing records.

Children

Amin is not directed at children under 13. Students under 18 should use it with a parent's or guardian's agreement.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before it takes effect.
