Common questions

Does Amin send my documents anywhere? No. They stay on your device, or on your own Amin computer if you use one.

Which devices can run the model? iPhones with 8 GB of memory (iPhone 15 Pro and later), iPads and Macs with Apple silicon.

How do I cancel Pro? In your Apple ID or Google Play subscriptions. Your plan stays active until the end of the period.

How do I delete my account? In the app, Settings › Account › Delete account, or see account deletion.
